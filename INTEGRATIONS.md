## Weather

`weather.py` now provides real Open-Meteo weather using a city-level location in
private configuration. Its fixed HTTPS endpoint disables proxies/redirects, bounds
responses to 1 MB, caches successes for fifteen minutes and backs off failures for
one minute. Current conditions are labelled model estimates rather than station
observations; no observation timestamp is invented. The UI shows twelve hours,
seven days, rain probability and available detail fields. Provider attribution is
shown in Weather. No severe-weather alert feed is configured yet.

Today fetches calendar and weather concurrently with one in-flight job per source
and a 4.5-second overall wait. Source outages preserve the clock, shopping and
quote, with clickable source alerts. The independent Weather/Calendar views retain
the existing browser last-good/stale behavior.
