Humblestead Home Hub · Product specification

Revised requirements v1.1 draft

Changes against v1.0 (12 Oct 2026), from the visual audit of the first mockups. Target: 10.1-inch landscape frame on a kitchen wall, read from 1–3 m by the whole family; custom web UI on an older Android WebView; data and credentials on the home server.

Confirmed: from v1.0 / owner Changed: differs from v1.0 New: not in v1.0

1 · Legibility & touch

  • Changed Touch targets ≥ 56 × 56 px (v1.0: 44): wet or busy kitchen hands. Whole list rows tappable.
  • New Type floor: clock ≥ 96 px, key values ≥ 28 px, body ≥ 20 px, nothing below 16 px.
  • New Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for all text, 3:1 for icons and borders. No light-grey secondary text; tinted chips carry dark text.
  • Confirmed Charcoal on warm white, one restrained blue accent, no unneeded animation.

2 · Navigation

  • Confirmed Fixed 180 px left sidebar, icon + short label, in this exact order on every screen: Today, Calendar, Shopping, Weather, Quotes, Photos, Smart Home, Settings. Each entry appears once.
  • Changed Quotes and Photos are separate views (the mockup merged them).
  • New Sidebar footer shows overall sync health; tapping it opens Settings.
  • New After 2 min idle on any tab, return to Today before the screensaver.

3 · Screensaver, wake & night

  • Confirmed 3 min idle → full-bleed Aura photo; swipe up wakes to Today (no passcode).
  • Changed Photo interval 30 s (v1.0 proposed 15 s): calmer and lighter on the frame.
  • New Clock sits on a soft bottom scrim; no page dots. Clock position drifts a few px each minute (burn-in).
  • New A tap shows "Swipe up to open" for 3 s; it never changes tabs.
  • Changed Night (quiet hours, default 22:00–06:00): dark clock at low brightness plus the next morning item; no photos. Settles open decision §9.

4 · Alert strip (global)

  • New One-line strip at the top of the main area, hidden when empty. Highest severity first, at most one line plus "+n more".
  • Sources only from the server allowlist: door contact open longer than [N] min, weather alert, sync failure, Home Assistant offline.
  • Tap opens the relevant tab. It replaces the optional smart-home status line on Today.
  • Never shows camera images or personal message content.

5 · Today

  • Confirmed Large clock and date, compact weather, next 2–4 events, shopping preview, one quote. No vertical scroll.
  • New Events show relative time ("Now", "in 25 min") and their calendar color.
  • New Weather tile adds the next rain window ("Rain from 3 PM").
  • Changed Quote becomes a one-line footer, not a hero card; the space goes to Next up.
  • Confirmed Shopping shows the open count plus up to 3 items.

6 · Calendar

  • Changed Default view is 3-day (Day, 3-day, Week, Month remain).
  • Confirmed Current-time line, all-day row, Today button, previous/next, date picker.
  • New The legend is the filter: each calendar is a toggle chip with its color. Colors differ in lightness as well as hue.
  • New Overlapping events sit side by side; visible window 7 AM–6 PM with scroll for other hours.

7 · Shopping

  • Confirmed Categories Produce, Dairy, Pantry, Household, Other; quantity and notes; large checkboxes; undo on delete.
  • New Quick-add chips for frequent items, next to the text field.
  • New Completed items collapse into one group with "Clear completed".
  • Confirmed Per-item sync state is shown: "Saving…", "Not saved · Retry". Never a false success.

8 · Weather

  • Confirmed Observed current conditions are labelled apart from forecast values, with source and update time.
  • Confirmed Hourly strip with chance of rain; 7-day cards with chance of rain or snow.
  • Changed Detail metrics (wind, humidity, UV, sun times) become a secondary grid; rain and temperature lead.
  • Confirmed Fields the provider omits are left out, never filled with placeholders.

9 · Smart Home: your devices only

  • Changed Thermostat, smart lock and garage door removed from the mocks: none are in the device inventory.
  • New Order: door-contact status (T110), then scene buttons (Good Morning, Movie Night, All Off), then room light tiles (Govee H6008, Twinkly), then cameras.
  • Confirmed Camera snapshots refresh every 30–60 s; at most one live view, opened on tap. The D205 doorbell stays "pending verification" until the integration is confirmed.
  • New Every command shows "Sending…" and then the state Home Assistant confirms, with a 5 s Undo for lights and scenes.

10 · Frame performance (WebView ~106, ~1 GB)

  • New No live backdrop-filter blur; the server pre-renders a blurred copy of each photo if one is needed.
  • New Fonts are self-hosted (two families, three weights at most); no web-font calls from the frame.
  • Confirmed Only one mounted view; unmount hidden tabs and camera tiles. Server-made thumbnails and display copies only.
  • New At most one shadow per card; crossfades only; budget of 200 KB JS gzipped.

11 · Architecture & privacy

  • New A dedicated dashboard backend on the home server holds a scoped Home Assistant token and the Aura secrets. The frame never talks to Home Assistant directly; HA keeps its private bridge and loopback UI.
  • New Camera snapshots and doorbell events stay in the dashboard backend; they never enter the Hermes assistant.
  • Confirmed No password or token in the URL, bundle, logs or responses to the frame.

12 · Settings & honest states

  • Confirmed Sync status per source with last success; display, screensaver and quiet-hours settings; diagnostics; admin path.
  • New Each source has three visible states: current, stale (amber, with age) and failed (with Retry). Stale data is never shown as live.
  • New The admin mode button requires the two-finger hold from §4.3; it is never a single tap.

Decisions taken in this draft

  • Wake always lands on Today.
  • Screensaver after 3 min; photo every 30 s.
  • Night is a dark dim clock, not screen-off: the frame is also the kitchen clock.
  • Shopping source: Home Assistant to-do (editable from the HA phone app too).

Still open

  • Calendar source: direct server-side Google sync (richer detail) or HA calendar entities?
  • Door-contact alert threshold [N] min, and which doors.
  • Calendar names and colors per family member.
  • Home location, units and quote categories.
  • Exact frame model and WebView version (phase 0).
